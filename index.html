Research interests

Four threads, connected by one question. Which part of a signal has to survive, and what is it worth spending to protect it.

01

Multimedia compression and processing

Coding, error concealment and quality preservation for images and video, including multi view coding and artifact analysis.

02

Multimedia communication over networks

Scalable video delivery, network coding, caching in content centric networks, and cross layer quality of service for wireless links.

03

Computer vision and image processing

Denoising, segmentation and detection, applied to medical imaging, industrial inspection and sign language recognition.

04

Machine learning and artificial intelligence

Representation learning, reinforcement learning and generative models, applied where the decision has a cost, such as clinical and emergency response settings.
