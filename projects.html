New artificial intelligence techniques to improve the quality of life of people who are deaf or without a larynx, through fingerspelling recognition and speech enhancement

National Research Council of Thailand. Principal investigator.
March 2024 to March 2027
Two populations, one problem. A person signs and is not read, or speaks without a larynx and is not understood. The work builds recognition for fingerspelled handshapes and enhancement for alaryngeal speech, and asks in both cases what a listener or a reader actually needs recovered rather than what a loss function finds easy.

Working together

Three quarters of these projects were commissioned rather than proposed. An agency or a company had a service that did not work well enough and needed the engineering done, and what they received was software that went into operation. If you have a signal that has to survive a lossy path and then be acted on by a person or a downstream system, that is the shape of problem this group works on.
